The festival

One stage.
Thousands of stories.

SIBĀQ is Darul Huda's national arts fest — a huge meeting point for students, ideas, performance, creativity and campus spirit.

More than a competition.

Across a packed programme of artistic, literary, cultural and performance events, students bring their strongest work to a shared national arena.
